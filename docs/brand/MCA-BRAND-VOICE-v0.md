## 1. Vocea într-o frază / Voice in one sentence

**RO**: ModulCA vorbește ca un prieten care a citit legea în locul tău: clar, calm, exact, cu articolul de lege la vedere și fără să se dea drept arhitectul sau avocatul tău.

**EN**: ModulCA sounds like a friend who read the law so you don't have to. We are clear, calm and exact, we show the article number, and we never pretend to be your architect or lawyer.

Four traits, in order of priority (when two conflict, the higher one wins):

| # | Trăsătură (RO) | Trait (EN) | Ce înseamnă concret |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Exact** | Accurate | Every limit, deadline or procedure has an article (art. 294, alin. 4). If we're not sure, we say so. |
| 2 | **Clar** | Clear | Short sentences. One idea per sentence. Explain the legal term the first time it appears. |
| 3 | **Calm / liniștitor** | Reassuring | The permit is a process, not a monster. No fear-selling („amenzi uriașe!"). |
| 4 | **Practic** | Practical | Every piece ends with „ce faci concret acum" plus the next step on modulca.eu. |

## 2. Audiențe / Audiences

Same brand, two registers. Each piece is written for **one** audience. NotebookLM's „focus" box always names it (strategy §6).

| | **Proprietar / Homeowner** (primary for content) | **Arhitect / Professional** (primary for B2B, Architect tier €49.99/mo) |
|---|---|---|
| Cine | 30–55 years old, owns or is buying land (often rural or peri-urban), wants a house, an annex or a renovation. Has never read the law. | Independent architect or small firm, 30–50 years old. Knows the old law (L.50/1991) and needs the CATUC changes fast. |
| Ce îl doare | „Îmi trebuie autorizație?", „Cât durează?", „Cât costă?", „Pe cine sun?" | „Ce s-a schimbat față de L.50?", „Ce conține acum PAC-ul?", „Cum folosesc notificarea pentru clienți?" |
| Adresare RO | **tu** (direct, warm). Formal e-mails and documents use **dumneavoastră**. | **dumneavoastră** in articles and e-mails, **tu** acceptable on LinkedIn / Shorts |
| Nivel tehnic | Zero jargon without an explanation. Concrete examples („o bucătărie de vară de 15 m²"). | Precise terms, article and paragraph numbers, comparison tables old vs new. |
| CTA tipic | „Verifică gratuit ce procedură îți trebuie → modulca.eu/autorizare" | „Generează pachetul de documente pentru clientul tău" / „Încearcă Architect tier" |
| Ton EN | Friendly, plain English, "you" | Professional, concise, still "you" |

**Rule**: never talk down to the homeowner, and never lecture the architect. The architect is a **partner** of the platform, not a competitor. We prepare drafts; they sign.

## 3. Vocabular / Vocabulary

### 3.1 Termeni corecți (obligatoriu) / Required terms

| ✅ Folosim | ❌ Nu folosim | Notă |
|---|---|---|
| **PAC** (proiect pentru autorizarea executării lucrărilor de construire) | DTAC | CATUC uses PAC (art. 262, 264, 265). „DTAC" appears only in an explicit „DTAC → PAC" explainer. |
| **notificare** / procedura de notificare | „autorizație simplificată", „aviz rapid" | art. 294–298. It is a distinct procedure, not a cheaper permit. |
| **certificat de urbanism** (CU) | „certificat de construcție", „aviz de urbanism" | art. 253–258 |
| **autorizație de construire** (AC) | „autorizație de construcție", „permis" (RO) | art. 259. EN: *building permit*. |
| **aprobare tacită** (15 zile lucrătoare) | „aprobare automată", „dacă tac, ai voie" | art. 296(1)(a). Works starts 15 calendar days after (art. 296(6)). Always state **both** deadlines. |
| **proiect tehnic de execuție cu conținut simplificat** | „schiță", „plan simplu" | Required for notification (art. 295(4)), done by architects and engineers with the right of signature |
| **arhitect cu drept de semnătură** | „un arhitect care semnează pe hârtie", „semnătură de complezență" | OAR deontology. We never suggest a courtesy signature. |
| **mediul rural / intravilan** | „la țară", „pe câmp" (in an explanation) | Colloquial is fine in a hook only if the next sentence says the legal term |
| **CATUC** (Codul Amenajării Teritoriului, Urbanismului și Construcțiilor, Legea 169/2026) | „noul cod" alone, „Legea 50 nouă" | Full name at first mention, then CATUC. In force since **25.08.2026**. |
| **casă modulară** | „container", „casă din container", „prefabricat ieftin" | Brand positioning |

EN glossary (first mention keeps the RO term in italics): *certificat de urbanism* (urban planning certificate) · *autorizație de construire* (building permit) · *notificare* (notification procedure) · *PAC* (permit design documentation) · *aprobare tacită* (tacit approval).

### 3.2 Cifre — doar verificate / Numbers: only verified ones

Use only figures that passed the §7 gate against `docs/references/catuc/catuc-169-2026-text.md` (see also `docs/research/2026-11-25-notebooklm-catuc-answers.md` → Verification). Examples, always with their conditions:

- Annex **without** any formality: ≤ 20 m² cumulated, ≤ 2,5 m height, **no foundation**, temporary and demountable, rural, wood or metal structure, respecting PUG/RLU (art. 299). Never say just „anexa sub 20 m² nu cere nimic".
- House by notification: **one** single-family house, P or D+P, no basement, ≤ 150 mp built area, rural intravilan, **only** on standard projects or individual projects by specialist teams, following the OAR rural architecture guide and approved by the county chief architect (art. 294). Never say just „casa de 150 mp fără autorizație".

**Never publish** (known NotebookLM errors, per Chat 2): AC validity „12–36 luni" (wrong: AC = 3 years, art. 309) · „art. 272" for the AC · „art. 143" as a local fee · a „24 aug 2026 cut-off" · order numbers Ord. 1022/2026 and 975/2026 (unverified).

## 4. Do / Don't

| ✅ DO (RO + EN) | ❌ DON'T |
|---|---|
| Start with the reader's question: „Vrei o bucătărie de vară? Iată când nu-ți trebuie autorizație." | Start with „Conform art. 299 alin. (1) lit. c)…" (put the article at the end of the sentence or on screen) |
| Give the article number on screen or in the caption | Give a limit without its conditions |
| Say „depinde de…" and list the 2–3 factors | Promise an outcome („sigur nu-ți trebuie autorizație") |
| Recommend the free checker and a real architect for anything that needs a signature | Suggest that ModulCA or AI replaces the architect (D4) |
| Admit uncertainty: „Formularul oficial de notificare încă nu e publicat prin ordin (art. 295(10))." | Invent forms, order numbers or fees |
| Disclose AI: „Narat cu voce generată AI, verificat de echipa ModulCA." | Present an AI voice as „arhitectul nostru" or any real person (§10.1 house rule 3) |
| Use concrete RO examples: rural plot, summer kitchen, attic conversion | Use fear: „Amenzi de mii de lei dacă nu știi asta!" |
| End with one CTA | Stack 3 CTAs, or use pushy sales copy in legal content |
| Use Romanian diacritics (ă, â, î, ș, ț — comma-below) | Write RO without diacritics or with cedilla ş/ţ |
| Use neutral examples for people | Name the partner architect, clients or any private person in public content without written consent |

**Emoji / format**: at most 1–2 emoji per social caption and none in articles or PDFs. Use title case only for proper names. Hashtags: `#CATUC #autorizatie #casamodulara #ModulCA` (max 5).

## 5. Disclaimer — formulări standard / Standard wording

Required on **every** public piece that touches law (§7 gate, step 2). Use the version that fits the format.

**Scurt (Shorts, caption, carousel) — RO**
> Informativ, nu consultanță juridică sau de proiectare. Verifică la primărie sau cu un arhitect. Sursa: CATUC (L.169/2026), art. ___ → modulca.eu/___

**Scurt — EN**
> For information only, not legal or design advice. Check with your city hall or an architect. Source: CATUC (Law 169/2026), art. ___ → modulca.eu/___

**Lung (video description, articol, PDF) — RO**
> Acest material are scop exclusiv informativ și nu înlocuiește consultanța de specialitate (arhitect cu drept de semnătură, jurist sau autoritatea emitentă). Informațiile reflectă Legea nr. 169/2026 (CATUC) la data publicării, ___. Normele de aplicare și formularele oficiale pot modifica detaliile. ModulCA nu răspunde pentru decizii luate exclusiv pe baza acestui material.

**Lung — EN**
> This material is for information only and does not replace professional advice (a licensed architect, a lawyer or the issuing authority). It reflects Law no. 169/2026 (CATUC) as of ___. Implementing rules and official forms may change the details. ModulCA is not liable for decisions based solely on this material.

**Linie AI (any NotebookLM-generated audio/video) — RO / EN**
> Narat cu voce generată AI (NotebookLM), verificat de echipa ModulCA. / Narrated with an AI-generated voice (NotebookLM), reviewed by the ModulCA team.

Plus: tick **„AI use"** in YouTube Studio (§10.1).

## 7. Checklist înainte de publicare / Pre-publish checklist

1. ☐ One audience (homeowner **or** architect), stated in the NotebookLM focus box
2. ☐ Every limit or deadline has an article and passed the fact check against the law text
3. ☐ Terms per §3.1 (PAC, notificare, certificat de urbanism, diacritics)
4. ☐ Disclaimer (short or long) plus a source link on modulca.eu
5. ☐ AI line plus YouTube „AI use" ticked, if AI voice or visuals are used
6. ☐ No partner-architect name, no private persons, no unverified order numbers
7. ☐ One CTA, and the modular-house bridge at most once
8. ☐ Flagship piece (long video, PDF guide)? Partner-architect review is done (§7 step 3)
